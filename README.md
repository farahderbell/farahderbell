## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🛡️ NashShield — Risk Simulation & Solvency II `2026`
> **Solvency Lab** — standard-formula computation and interpretation of the **SCR**, with ready-to-run shock scenarios across the 6 modules of the Solvency II "octopus" (Market, Counterparty Default, Life, Health, Non-Life, Operational).
> **Nash Module** — stochastic claims simulation (**Monte Carlo, GBM**) with reinsurance mechanisms (quota-share) and strategic optimization via **Nash equilibrium**.
> `Python` `Flask` `JavaScript`

### 🎗️ Breast Cancer Detection — ML & DL `2025`

> `Java/JavaFX` `Symfony` `MySQL`
